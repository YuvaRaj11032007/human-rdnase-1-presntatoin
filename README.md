# Cystic Fibrosis & Recombinant Human DNase 1 Interactive Presentation

An interactive medical and biological presentation exploring Cystic Fibrosis (CF), autosomal recessive inheritance, pathophysiology, NETosis, mucus viscosity, and therapeutic intervention with Recombinant Human DNase 1 (Pulmozyme / Genentech).

## 📋 Presentation Features

- **Interactive Canvas Visualizations**:
  - Autosomal Recessive Inheritance & Chromosome 7 (Hi Nanna Case Study)
  - Airway & Lung Anatomy and Physiology
  - Neutrophil Extracellular Trap (NETosis) formation and DNA-mucus viscosity
  - Genentech breakthrough discovery & Recombinant Human DNase 1 mechanism of action
- **Presenter & Audio Controls**:
  - Anatomical labels toggle (`L`)
  - Speaker presentation notes drawer (`N`)
  - Sound effects toggle (`M`)
  - Fullscreen mode (`F`)
  - Smooth slide navigation controls (Arrow keys / Space / Page navigation)
